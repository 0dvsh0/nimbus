Questions

Asked & answered.

Does Nimbus send messages for me?

Never. Nimbus only suggests. Nothing is typed until you press Tab, and nothing is sent until you hit send yourself.

Which sites does it work on?

X, LinkedIn, WhatsApp Web, Instagram, Gmail, Slack, Discord, Reddit, Messenger, Telegram Web, YouTube comments and GitHub — plus, on Storm, any text box on the web.

What happens to my conversations?

The visible thread is used, in-flight, to write a suggestion — then it's gone. We don't store messages, sell data, or train models on your conversations.

Will Tab still work normally?

Yes. Tab only accepts when a ghost suggestion is visible. Otherwise it does exactly what it always did. You can also remap the key in settings.

Can I turn it off on certain sites?

One click on the cloud in your toolbar pauses Nimbus for that site, for an hour, or forever.
